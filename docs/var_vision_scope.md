# Scoping a VAR-style vision model: what official systems do, what xg-lab has, and what to build

Date: 2026-10-14. Prepared for Alejandro (xg-lab owner). Status: draft, not committed.

Verification convention used throughout: **(verified)** = read today from a primary file (GitHub README, Hawk-Eye PDF, StatsBomb JSON/PDF, or computed from downloaded data); **(verified, search)** = confirmed today from web-search summaries of the primary page or of two or more reputable secondary sources (FIFA, Premier League, Genius Sports, Football España, Forbes, ESPN, arXiv, Nature) where the primary page itself is DNS-blocked from this sandbox; **(unverified)** = recalled from training or taken from a single press source that could not be opened today; **(unverified, post-cutoff)** = no recall to lean on either. The research agents could fetch only github.com, pypi.org, raw.githubusercontent.com and s3.amazonaws.com; a second pass on 2026-10-14 with web search upgraded the items now tagged (verified, search).

## 1. Short answer

No: not with xg-lab data, nor with any single broadcast camera. Official VAR is three different problems: offside and goal-line are multi-camera metrology (at FIFA events 12 to 16 dedicated roof cameras tracking 29 body points at 50 Hz plus a 500 Hz ball sensor, verified by search; the Premier League and La Liga run their own multi-camera optical systems, with camera counts and frame rates reported inconsistently in the press); fouls, penalties and red cards are human judgement from replays; the referee always decides. StatsBomb open data has zero pixels, no continuous tracking, no limbs, no kick timing and no VAR/review field (verified from 187,924 Euro 2024 events).

What you can build honestly: (a) calibrated refereeing-decision models on StatsBomb 360 that extend xg-lab directly; (b) a 2D offside geometry engine with uncertainty, validated on open tracking data and then on 360 freeze frames; (c) a broadcast offside-line estimator with metre-level error bars on SoccerNet-GSR; (d) a calibrated, abstaining foul classifier on SoccerNet-MVFoul as a stretch. (c) and (d) need SoccerNet video, Python/PyTorch and one consumer GPU.

## 2. How official VAR / SAOT / goal-line tech actually works

The IFAB protocol (unverified; consistent with recall): VAR assists only for a "clear and obvious error" or "serious missed incident" in four categories (goal/no goal including offside, penalty/no penalty, direct red card, mistaken identity). Every incident gets a silent check; only the referee can start a review; the referee makes the final decision. [IFAB](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/video-assistant-referee-var-protocol/)

Process figures: PL VAR errors by the KMI panel 38 (2022/23), 31 (2023/24), 18 (2024/25), 25 (2025/26) (verified, search; the 31 rests on one BBC report relayed by a second outlet); PL per-match VAR delay 64 s to 39 s in 2024/25 (unverified); La Liga SAOT cost around EUR 5m (single-outlet press, unverified). Two flagged public incidents. Real Sociedad vs Barcelona (Nov 2024): Lewandowski ruled off by a toe; Barcelona and the press argued the system confused his feet with Aguerd's (verified by search as a disputed case; the limb-registration explanation is itself unverified). Newcastle vs Man City (Carabao Cup semi-final, 13 Jan 2026): SAOT could not be used because of the number of players in the box, officials drew manual crosshairs, and the review took 5 to 6 minutes; the Premier League calls these "edge cases" (verified, search). That second incident is the clearest public evidence that even the official optical system abstains under occlusion and falls back to humans.

### Why it cannot be reproduced from one broadcast camera

- **Physics.** At 25 fps an 8 m/s sprinter moves 32 cm per frame (16 cm at 50 fps, 1.6 cm at 500 Hz); a 25 m/s shot moves 0.5 to 1 m per frame at 25 to 50 fps. So one frame of kick-point ambiguity already exceeds the 5 cm manual-line tolerance and the 10 cm alert threshold the pro systems reportedly use (both unverified). A homography maps ground-contact points only; the furthest shoulder or knee is often airborne. At a main-camera far touchline, the calibration community's own 5 px tolerance (verified) is tens of centimetres on grass, and naive automatic calibration gives a 1.17 m median offside-line error (BallHawk README, self-reported, read today). The best peer-reviewed single-camera attempt found today, a YOLOv8 system in Scientific Reports (May 2026), reached 83.0% offside-decision accuracy (95% CI 79 to 87) on 240 test instances; its authors state this is below professional benchmarks and limited to proof of concept by single-camera occlusion and fast movement (verified, search).
- **Data.** StatsBomb has zero pixels. Its 360 frames are event-sampled 2D snapshots with mean 16.4 of 22 players visible and the keeper absent in 62.6% of frames (verified). No key path among 207 in 187,924 Euro 2024 events records a VAR review or overturn (verified). No open dataset with VAR decisions was found anywhere; this is a negative that cannot be proven.
- **Legal.** Scraping PL or La Liga broadcasts is copying copyrighted broadcasts. UK CDPA s.29A covers only non-commercial research with lawful access (recalled, not re-read). SoccerNet is the only lawful video source found.

## 5. Candidate scopes

Three designs were drafted and scored by an independent judge on feasibility, data availability, answers the user's question, portfolio value and honesty of scope (each out of 10, total out of 50).

| Design | Feas. | Data | Answers Q | Portfolio | Honesty | Total | Two-line verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. mvp-first | 8 | 7 | 6 | 7 | 9 | 37 | Tight and executable, but touches none of the repo's data and drops offside, which is what most people mean by SAOT. Best idea: the abstaining "check" output. |
| B. fidelity-first | 3 | 5 | 7 | 5 | 8 | 28 | The only route to centimetre ground truth, but a 30-week, USD 4 to 15k hardware project for a solo builder. Best ideas: Law-11 engine with Monte-Carlo uncertainty, per-layer error budgets. |
| C. xg-lab-leverage | 8 | 9 | 8 | 9 | 9 | **43** | Layered so each layer ships on its own; uses data already in the repo first; honest about where vision stops. Weak spots: untested thresholds, stale GSR baseline. |

**A. mvp-first (VARcheck-MVP).** A calibrated, abstaining foul/card classifier on SoccerNet-MVFoul: frozen or lightly fine-tuned Apache-2.0 video backbone (MViT-v2-S, X3D-M or DINOv2 features), multi-view pooling, two heads (8 foul types, 4 offence/severity classes), temperature or isotonic calibration on the 411-clip val split, and a reject option that outputs NO ACTION, CLEAR AND OBVIOUS or REFER TO ON-FIELD REVIEW. About 7.5 weeks on one 12 to 24 GB GPU; evaluated on the 301-action test split and one submission to the official challenge server. Expected combined balanced accuracy in the 40 to 48 band (published SOTA 52.22, the 2025 challenge winner, verified by search). Contributes nothing from xg-lab's data; transfers only its calibration discipline. Explicitly never draws offside lines.

**B. fidelity-first (Open-SAOT).** A six-camera fixed rig at a local pitch (Tier A: GoPros at 4K/60 and 2.7K/120; Tier B: global-shutter machine-vision cameras with hardware trigger), ChArUco plus surveyed pitch-marking bundle adjustment, RF-DETR/ByteTrack per view, RTMPose 2D pose, cross-view triangulation to 26-joint 3D skeletons, ballistic-trajectory kick point, a Law-11 engine with Monte-Carlo uncertainty, and a human-in-the-loop review app. Ground truth from 200+ staged kicks with laser-measured body positions and 4+ amateur matches with 3-referee consensus labels. 30 weeks, ~450 GB per match, consent and GDPR process. Would give a genuinely SAOT-like instrument at 5 to 10 cm, never VAR.

**C. xg-lab-leverage (VAR-Lab).** Three layers: L1 decision models in R inside the repo (P(card | foul), P(penalty | foul in box), referee random intercepts with shrinkage, split-half persistence, full calibration suite); L2 a 2D offside geometry engine on 360 freeze frames whose fitted P(offside call | margin) curve doubles as a psychometric estimate of 360 positional noise; L3 a separate Python package running a permissive-licence SoccerNet-GSR pipeline (RF-DETR or RT-DETR, ByteTrack, SigLIP teams, TrackLab calibration, RTMPose) that outputs pitch coordinates and an offside line with bootstrap intervals, scored by a Python port of xg-lab's calibration code; L3b the MV-Foul classifier as a stretch. Laptop for L1/L2, one 12 GB GPU for L3.

### Recommended scope: C with grafts from A and B

Take design C and graft in: (1) A's abstention output (NO ACTION / CLEAR AND OBVIOUS / REFER TO ON-FIELD REVIEW) with a coverage-vs-accuracy curve on every probabilistic output; (2) A's foul-track milestones and one official challenge submission; (3) A's day-1 SoccerNet access resolution, own loaders to avoid GPL-3 code, gated terms quoted verbatim; (4) A's game-disjoint leakage check; (5) B's Law-11 engine as a pure function with Monte-Carlo propagation (perturb positions and kick time, output margin distribution, 90% interval, P(offside)); (6) B's validation on IDSSE and SkillCorner before touching 360; (7) B's per-layer error-budget table; (8) B's frame-rate arithmetic; (9) B's rig as an appendix only; (10) report every metric stratified by visibility, with coverage.

## 7. Evaluation plan

| Layer | Primary metric | Secondary | Ground truth |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 card / penalty | OOF log loss and Brier vs base rate; ECE (10 bins); reliability plot; Brier decomposition | Referee-held-out drop; AUC; intercept SD and shrinkage; persistence r with permutation p; coverage-vs-accuracy with abstention | StatsBomb `card`/`penalty` = referee's final call, post-VAR |
| L2 offside geometry | Agreement with referee call vs |margin|; AUC of margin; psychometric width | Calibration of P(offside call); sensitivity to keeper missing, receiver outside visible area | IDSSE/SkillCorner logged offside events; StatsBomb `Pass Offside` |
| L3 pitch coordinates | GS-HOTA on GSR valid (5 m kernel, verified) | Median pitch error in metres per role and depth band; HOTA; JaC@5 | GSR ground-truth positions |
| L3 offside line | Median absolute line error (m); 90% interval coverage and width | Sign agreement vs |margin|; error vs zoom and players visible | Line derived from GSR positions |
| L3b foul | Balanced accuracy per head (test split); challenge-server score | ECE, reliability, coverage curve, confidence vs referee disagreement | MV-Foul referee labels (one annotator) |

All confidence intervals are bootstrapped over matches (L1/L2) or clips (L3), never over events, extending the match-grouped discipline in `make_folds()`. `calib_scores()`, `reliability_data()` and `isotonic_recalibrate()` from `R/04_calibration.R` and `log_loss()`, `brier_score()`, `expected_calibration_error()`, `brier_decomposition()` from `R/utils.R` are reused unchanged in R and ported with parity tests for Python.

**Benchmarking against official decisions, what is and is not possible.** StatsBomb labels in the 360 competitions are the final call after any VAR review (all of those competitions ran VAR, recalled, unverified), but no field records which calls were reviewed or overturned (verified), so the models target "the system's final decision" and the review history is unrecoverable. No machine-readable VAR-error dataset exists; PL KMI counts and La Liga CTA figures are prose. Plan: a hand-curated 50 to 100 incident table from public VAR-review columns as a qualitative appendix only, since no video is legally available to run on them. The honesty table puts official tolerances (5 cm manual line, 10 cm alert, 7 cameras per goal, "millimetre accurate") next to our numbers in metres and does not round the gap away.

## 8. Risks, limits and legal

- **Broadcast rights.** No scraping of PL, La Liga or YouTube. The PL is litigating piracy and lobbying on AI training (unverified press). UK CDPA s.29A permits only non-commercial TDM with lawful access (recalled, not re-read).
- **SoccerNet terms.** The devkit README lists MV-Foul, GSR and Tracking annotations as public on Hugging Face and raw broadcast video as gated by an access form; NDA now applies only to BAS/PCBAS repos (verified). The sn-mvfoul README still says NDA (verified contradiction). The gated-repo terms were not readable today: read and quote them before publishing weights or clips. Raw tracking videos are still EXRCS-only (verified).
- **Code licences.** sn-gamestate and sn-mvfoul are GPL-3.0 (verified): write your own loaders so the pipeline stays MIT/Apache. Ultralytics YOLO is AGPL-3.0 (verified) and is excluded. **What this will never do that real VAR does:** make or recommend a decision in a sanctioned match; goal-line decisions; centimetre offside lines or kick-point timing; interference, deliberate-play, handball or mistaken-identity judgement; real-time operation; any "VAR-grade" or FIFA Quality claim; learning from or auditing actual VAR reviews, because none are public. Do not name the project in a way that implies replacement of VAR.
